# Presenter's Notes

## Key teaching points

- Storyboard first, slides second.
- Treat AI as a presentation strategist, not just a slide generator.
- Prompt quality improves when audience, decision, source, format, and tone are clear.
- Executive decks should support decisions, not just explain topics.
- Always review AI output for facts, assumptions, logic, and tone.

## Suggested closing line

"The future PowerPoint skill is not slide formatting. It is directing AI to build a clear business argument."
