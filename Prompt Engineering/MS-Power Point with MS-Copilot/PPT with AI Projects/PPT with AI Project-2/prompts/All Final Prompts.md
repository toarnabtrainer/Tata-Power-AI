```text
Deck/storyboard:

Slide 1: Customer Churn Requires Immediate Leadership Action
Main message: Churn has increased from 8% to 13%, creating risk to recurring revenue.
Bullets:
- Churn has risen across the last two quarters.
- Revenue predictability is under pressure.
- A focused 90-day response is required.
Visual: KPI headline showing churn movement from 8% to 13%.

Slide 2: Rising Churn Is Creating Revenue Retention Risk
Main message: The churn increase directly affects ARR stability and growth efficiency.
Bullets:
- More revenue is now at risk from existing customers.
- Retention issues may increase pressure on new sales.
- Leadership action is needed before churn becomes structural.
Visual: ARR-at-risk bar or waterfall chart.

Slide 3: Onboarding, Adoption, and Support Are the Main Churn Drivers
Main message: Churn is being driven by controllable customer experience gaps.
Bullets:
- Incomplete onboarding weakens early customer confidence.
- Low product usage signals poor value realization.
- Support delays increase dissatisfaction.
Visual: Root-cause tree or driver diagram.

Slide 4: Churn Risk Is Concentrated in Specific Customer Segments
Main message: Retention efforts should prioritize the highest-risk customer groups.
Bullets:
- Mid-market accounts show elevated churn risk.
- New customers need stronger first-60-day engagement.
- Low-usage accounts require proactive outreach.
Visual: Segment risk heat map.

Slide 5: A 90-Day Plan Can Reduce Preventable Churn
Main message: A short, focused plan can address the most urgent churn drivers.
Bullets:
- Strengthen onboarding and adoption milestones.
- Implement customer health scoring.
- Activate retention playbooks for high-risk accounts.
Visual: 30-60-90 day roadmap.

Slide 6: Expected Impact Is Improved Retention and Revenue Predictability
Main message: The plan is designed to protect recurring revenue and improve customer engagement.
Bullets:
- Reduce avoidable customer exits.
- Improve renewal readiness.
- Increase visibility into churn-risk accounts.
Visual: Before-after impact view.

Slide 7: Clear Ownership Is Required Across Functions
Main message: Execution depends on coordinated ownership across Customer Success, Sales, Support, and Product.
Bullets:
- Customer Success owns health scoring and outreach.
- Sales supports renewal-risk accounts.
- Product and Support address adoption and service gaps.
Visual: RACI-style ownership table.

Slide 8: Leadership Approval Is Needed to Launch the Plan
Main message: The team needs approval to begin the 90-day churn reduction program.
Bullets:
- Approve the proposed retention plan.
- Confirm owners and review cadence.
- Align on success metrics and reporting.
Visual: Decision checklist.
```
