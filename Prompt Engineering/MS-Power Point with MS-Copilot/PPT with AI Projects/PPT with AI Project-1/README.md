## 🔴 Prompt 1: Create the Base Executive Presentation

```text
Create an executive presentation from the attached Ecommerce_Sales_Data.xlsx.

The workbook contains order-level data with these fields:
OrderID, CustomerName, Email, Product, Category, Price, Quantity, OrderDate, City, Country, PaymentMethod, OrderStatus.

Objective:
Create a leadership-ready ecommerce sales performance review with clear insights and actionable recommendations.

Analyze and calculate:
- Total revenue = Price × Quantity
- Average order value
- Total units sold
- Total order count
- Revenue and units by Product
- Revenue share by Category
- Revenue by City and geographic concentration
- Quarterly and yearly revenue trend, converting OrderDate serial values into real dates
- Order status mix: Delivered, Returned, Shipped, Cancelled, Pending
- Return + cancellation rate
- Payment method distribution

Create around 9 slides:
1. Title slide
2. Executive snapshot with KPI cards
3. Revenue by product
4. Category mix
5. Geography / revenue by city
6. Quarterly revenue trend
7. Order fulfillment / status breakdown
8. Payment methods
9. Key takeaways and recommendations

Each data slide should have one clear chart and one specific insight.
Use only figures calculated from the dataset. Do not invent numbers.
Format currency in ₹.
Keep city, product, category, and payment method names exactly as shown in the data.

Design style:
Clean modern corporate look, deep navy and teal palette, white content cards, crisp charts, and strong number callouts.
Use dark styling for the title and closing slides, and light styling for content slides.
```
